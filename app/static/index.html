How it works

  1. Image in. Any single-band orbital image: PNG/JPG/TIFF in the browser, or a Chandrayaan-2 PDS4 product via the command line (scripts/prepare_chandrayaan.py). 16-bit data is contrast-stretched; very large images are downscaled to 2048 px and the resolution is corrected automatically.
  2. U-Net. A 1.9 M-parameter U-Net predicts, for every pixel, the probability that it lies on a crater rim. Big images are processed in overlapping 256 px tiles and as an image pyramid so large craters are also found.
  3. Ring extraction. Ring templates of many radii are matched against the probability map; the best non-overlapping matches become craters (centre, radius, confidence).
  4. Science. Diameters are converted to metres, then the app computes crater density, the area covered by craters and the cumulative size-frequency distribution with a power-law slope fit.
  5. Landing screening. Every pixel gets a score from its distance to the nearest crater hazard and local roughness; the best separated spots that fit the lander footprint are proposed.

Training data

Labelled Chandrayaan-2 crater masks are not openly available, so the network is trained on procedurally simulated lunar terrain: fractal regolith, power-law crater populations with fresh/degraded bowl profiles (depth ≈ 0.2 D), and Lambertian shading under a random low sun. Ground truth is therefore exact, and the test scenes use seeds never seen in training.

Limitations

  • Accuracy numbers come from simulated test scenes. Performance on real TMC-2/OHRC images has not been measured against an expert catalogue.
  • Craters smaller than about 6 px across (r < 3 px) are not detected; very large craters need the image to be at least 2-4× their diameter.
  • The landing map is a teaching aid, not a mission tool: real site selection uses DEMs, slope maps and boulder counts.

Get real Chandrayaan-2 data

Register at the ISRO Science Data Archive (PRADAN), download a TMC-2 or OHRC calibrated product, then run python scripts/prepare_chandrayaan.py path/to/product.xml to make browser-sized PNG tiles.
